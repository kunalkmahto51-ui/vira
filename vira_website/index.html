ABOUT VIRA

Designed Around Clean Manufacturing.

VIRA follows an integrated manufacturing approach where water treatment, bottle preparation, filling, sealing and packaging work together through controlled production stages.

The focus remains simple — maintain consistency, hygiene and dependable product quality throughout the manufacturing journey.

From water processing to finished packaging — every stage matters.
